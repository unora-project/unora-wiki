# Pretty Flower

## Rewards

- ?
- Receives a Legend Mark

## Walkthrough

1. Speak to Jelus in Asilon (19,20).
2. Head west into Asilon Prairie and towards 64,185.  Walk around until a blade appears in your inventory.
3. Return to Jelus, and he will ask you to speak to Akum in the Equipment Shop.
4. Akum will ask you for 10 Polished Aerium and 100,000 gold to refine the blade. \
***(Raw Aerium can be found in Asilon Prairie, but must be refined in Tagor)***
5. After collecting the Refined Blade from Akum, return to Jelus.
6. Jelus will give you flowers.  Return to where you found the sword and place them under you.
7. Finally, return to Jelus and complete the quest.
