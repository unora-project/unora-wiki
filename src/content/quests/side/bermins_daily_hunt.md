# Bermin's Daily Hunt

## Rewards

- 1,500 Ability
- 20,000,000 Experience
- Receives a Legend Mark

## Walkthrough

1. Speak to Bermin in Asilon (12,8).
2. He will ask you to go hunt down 50x monsters in Asilon Prairie;
 - Green Blob;
 - Green Scrummel or;
 - Verdant Wingtoads
3. After killing enough, head back to Bermin and let him know.
