# Scravelmaw Hunt

## Rewards

- 500 Ability
- 10,000,000 Experience
- Receives a Legend Mark

## Walkthrough

1. Speak to Bermin in Asilon (12,8).
2. He will give you Bermin's Gold Harp.
3. Head out to Asilon Prairie 50,130.
4. Use the harp in your inventory to summon scravelmaw. \
***(You may need a group for this, if only to make it faster)***
5. After killing Scravelmaw, return to Bermin.
