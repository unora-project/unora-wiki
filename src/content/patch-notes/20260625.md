🎮 New Arena Game Modes
🎨 Paintball: Team Deathmatch
Four teams battle it out in a fast-paced Team Deathmatch!

First team to 25, 50, or 100 kills wins.
Paintball Skill range is 10 tiles.
10-second respawn timer.
Players spawn with 25 paintballs.

👑 Paintball: King of the Hill
Fight for control of the hill!

First team to 100, 200, or 300 points wins.
The hill relocates every 30 points.
Players spawn with 25 paintballs.

🏳️ Elixir Wars
Dye your enemies your team's color and secure victory!

Win by:

Capturing the enemy flag.
Eliminating the opposing team.

Rules

If you're dyed the enemy team's color, don't move.
Teammates can restore your color by shooting you with their own arrows.
Each player receives 25 arrows.
The enemy flag is located in the top corner of their spawn. Capture it and return it to your own spawn to win.
Remaining dyed the enemy team's color for 30 seconds will eliminate you automatically.

🐺 Wolf Hunt
Protect your captain!

Each team selects a Captain.
The Captain rides a mount and must keep moving.
If the wolf stops for more than 10 seconds, that team loses.
The wolf cannot be trapped—only the Captain can.

💣 Bomberman
A four-team explosive free-for-all!

Destroy boxes with bombs to reveal power-ups.
Current power-ups:

  
💣 Bomb Capacity
🔥 Bomb Blast Radius
Power-ups are stored in your inventory and can be activated whenever you choose.
Eliminate opponents—or bait them into their own explosions!
