Religion

- Mass has been reworked.
- Apostles must host mass in a roleplaying form. Cooldown on mass for casting it is 24 hours.
- Only Champion's of that religion may be promoted to Apostle.
- Apostles may appoint other Champions to Apostle as long as they understand the rules of casting Mass. Rules may be found on the Religion Board.
- Apostles may now cast Geas on players, Geas is a player run quest given by the Apostles, you may receive Geas once every 45 days.
- Apostles may only cast Geas once every 72 hours. 
- Apostles may remove players from the Temple, it is a 1 hour ban. 
- You may attend Mass once every 24 hours. 
- The Goddess will no longer host mass. 
- Added a way to get Deity Stone restored if you lose yours somehow. Cost 100 Essence of that Goddess. 

Mounts

- Mounts have been reworked to no longer use monster forms. 
- **Known bug- While mounted players are missing their necks, this will be fixed in a future patch, please refrain from bug reporting this.
- Mounts now use Item displays, allowing players to emote and customize their appearance while mounted.
- Mount speed is no longer locked to the mount, you are equally fast on all mounts you use. Mount Speed changes upon unlocking faster mounts (White Horse - Tier 1 / Kelberoth Tier 3/Ant or Dunan increases you to Tier 4)

Monk Sub Master Weapon

- Tilian Claw is changed to Tilian Obsidian (Green Obsidian)
