[Abilities]
- rewrote most of the ability descriptions for clarity and consistency

- many meall/aoe abilities have been changed to use ground targeting
- devour missing health heal changed from 20% per target to 15% per target
- cloud fist now correctly deals forced wind damage
- dragon breath now correctly deals forced fire damage
- fixed ao dall panel sprite

- potions have been rebalanced to heal/restore more flat amount, but less percentage (there will be better potions in med)

- arena hosts can now create reward medals from their trinket
- enchanting trinket now teleports you inside the enchanted haven
- the guild bank item order should now match that of the personal bank
